# Tiered Arsenal changelog

## 1.0.1 - Annihilator test weapon
- New creative-only Annihilator for testing spawns and high-health mobs. It has no recipe.
- A hit deals 1,000,000 damage with no attack cooldown. Kills count as yours, so drops and XP work.
- Right-click kills every monster within 32 blocks.
- Sneak + right-click kills every mob within 32 blocks, except players and tamed pets.
- It never breaks and won't break blocks if you miss.

## 1.0.0 - Release
- Balance pass:
  - Dagger attack speed 3.0 → 2.2; it was doing about 1.5x a sword's damage per second.
  - War hammer 0.6 → 0.8.
  - Greatsword 1.0 → 1.1.
- Tiered Industries and JEI are listed as optional dependencies.
- Checked for dedicated servers: no client-only code is reachable on a server.
- Checked every item, model, texture, name, recipe, loot table, tag and advancement for consistency.

## 0.9.0 - Enchantments, trims, quiver, tool belt and a boss
- Enchantments:
  - Shockwave (war hammer)
  - Assassin (dagger)
  - Reach (spear)
  - Counter (any shield)
- Cobalt, Titanium, Infernium and Aetherium ingots work as armour trim materials.
- Quiver: holds 4 stacks of arrows and feeds your bow when you run out.
- Tool belt: holds 4 tools; G swaps the held tool.
- Aetherium Guardian boss, summoned with a Void Sigil in the End:
  - Drops the Guardian Core, which makes the new Celestial module.
  - "Guardian Slain" advancement.

## 0.8.0 - Tools, crossbows, loot and more modules
- Pickaxe, shovel and hoe in every tier.
- Tiered crossbows.
- Explosive, fire and heavy arrows.
- Modules: Thorns, Regeneration, Swiftness, Auto-Smelt, Vein Miner.
- Ingots, templates and modules in structure chests.
- Mobs that spawn in tiered gear.
- Gunpowder recipe.

## 0.7.0 - Art and JEI
- Each ore has its own pattern.
- Cobalt and up get studs, gems or gold trim.
- JEI info pages for every item.

## 0.6.0 - Shields, throwing spears and polish
- Tiered shields with blocking perks.
- Throwable spears.
- Config file.
- Advancement tab.

## 0.5.0 - Gear modules
- Armoury Workbench (Forge Energy through the bottom face).
- Eight removable modules, with 1 to 5 slots by tier.

## 0.4.0 - Tiered Industries machines
- Dusts, plus Pulverizer, Ore Purifier and Alloy Smelter recipes.
- With TI installed, Titanium and up are made only in the Alloy Smelter.
- TI-alloy upgrade templates.

## 0.3.0 - New weapon types
- Greatsword, dagger, spear, war hammer and bow in every tier plus iron.

## 0.2.0 - Tiered upgrades
- Smithing chain Iron → Cobalt → Titanium → Infernium → Aetherium.
- Upgrade templates.

## 0.1.0 - Ores and base gear
- Cobalt, Titanium, Infernium and Aetherium ores.
- Sword, axe and armour in six materials.
- Set bonuses.
