# Tiered Industries

**Power your world one tier at a time** - from a coal generator on day one to a multiblock fusion reactor pushing hundreds of thousands of FE per tick.

Everything runs on **Forge Energy (FE)**, so Tiered Industries plugs straight into Mekanism, Thermal, Powah, AE2, Refined Storage and the rest of your pack.

## Generate
- **Coal Generator** - burns any furnace fuel.
- **Solar Panels** (4 tiers) and a **Wind Turbine** that rewards building high.
- **Boiler + Steam Turbine** - lava-heated, water from an endless Sink, steam piped anywhere.
- **Fission Reactor** - uranium fuel rods, real heat management, water cooling that makes bonus steam, automatic SCRAM.
- **Fusion** - a compact reactor, then a multiblock reactor of any size where every Magnet Coil adds output.

## Store and move
- **Battery Boxes** (4 tiers) with per-face Input / Output / Disabled, and the **Energy Bank** multiblock for trillions of FE.
- **Cables** that work as a network - length never matters.
- **Fluid, Gas and Item Pipes** with pull connections, filters (by item, tag or mod) and priorities. Watch items glide through glass tubes.

## Process and automate
- Ore **doubling** (Pulverizer) and **tripling** (Ore Purifier), Alloy Smelter, Compressor, Sawmill, Rock Crusher, Freezer.
- **Quarry** with size, depth, Silk Touch / Fortune and a void filter; **Crop Farmer**; **Auto-Crafter**; **Chunk Loader**.
- Every machine: Speed and Efficiency upgrades, redstone control, auto-output.

## Gear
- Electric Drills (3x3), a tree-felling Chainsaw, Jetpacks, Portable Batteries and a Wireless Charger.

## Friendly
- **In-game guidebook** with live recipes, **JEI** recipe pages, **Jade** tooltips, a **Wrench**, and a config file for balancing.

Requires Minecraft 1.20.1 and Forge 47+. JEI and Jade are optional.
