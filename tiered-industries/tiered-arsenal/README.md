# Tiered Arsenal

Weapons and armour for **Minecraft 1.20.1 / Forge 47+**, by **BobRoflza** - the companion mod to [Tiered Industries](../tiered-industries/).

- New ores for mid-to-high end game gear, plus weapons and armour from existing ores.
- A tiered system: each tier of weapon and armour is built up from the tier below.
- Designed to work hand in hand with Tiered Industries' machines.

## Download
Releases are tagged `tieredarsenal-v<version>` on the [Releases](https://github.com/CPSDomain/Modding/releases) page - put the jar in your `mods` folder.

## Guide
Tiered Arsenal is covered in the Tiered Industries guide, alongside the machines that make its gear.

## Building
`./gradlew build` - the jar lands in `build/libs/`. Pushing to `main` builds it on GitHub automatically, and a new `mod_version` publishes a Release.
